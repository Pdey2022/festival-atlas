About this atlas

Living traditions, transparent sources

Our first collection has 62 festival guides, with 62 expanded cultural guides. Festivals can belong to several states; associations shown are examples, not exhaustive cultural boundaries.

The annual calendar is a sourced starter collection, not a complete national schedule. A date is only shown when a source has been recorded for that year. Missing dates stay unconfirmed; they are never inferred from last year's calendar.

Your saved collection

Favourites and your trip itinerary (including dates and notes) are stored in this browser only, with no account or server database. Clearing browser data removes them. Shared collection links contain the festival IDs you selected; anyone with the link can view that selection. Nothing is shared until you choose to copy a link. Offline downloads use this browser’s cache to save app files and selected public guides; optional photos are fetched from Wikimedia. Removing offline downloads keeps your favourites and trip notes. Browsers may evict stored data, so keep a JSON backup. Download favourites from the Saved page and your itinerary from My trip. Trip backups and printouts contain your notes; they are not included in shared collection links.

We do not add analytics. GitHub Pages and external services apply their own policies. Viewing a photograph contacts Wikimedia; following source, map or correction links opens the named external service.
