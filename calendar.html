How date checks work

Sources are checked manually. Upcoming and ongoing listings need a date recheck after 90 days, or after 30 days when the event starts within the next 30 days. Provisional listings always need organiser confirmation. The Needs review filter includes both. Past listings are archived; dates are never rolled forward automatically.

Labels use today’s date in India and your device clock, including offline. A recent check is not a guarantee that the programme is unchanged.
