端午节

Dragon Boat Festival

Duānwǔ Jié

Boat racing, rice dumplings and regional remembrance traditions bring communities together.

May–June · typical season

The story

Dragon Boat Festival combines sporting, food and remembrance customs. UNESCO highlights its importance along the middle and lower Yangtze and describes traditions that differ between communities. Qu Yuan is remembered in Hubei and Hunan; the people and stories honoured elsewhere can differ.

What you may experience

  • Public dragon-boat races where local organisers provide visitor access.
  • Regional performances and gatherings associated with the festival.
  • Rice dumplings and household customs that vary across communities.

Food & shared tables

Zongzi are wrapped rice dumplings associated with the festival. Ask vendors about fillings and preparation; recipes are regional.

Visiting thoughtfully

Editorial planning suggestions

  • Watch from designated spectator areas and respect waterside barriers.
  • Do not assume a race is open to casual participation.
  • Follow the organiser’s weather, access and cancellation updates.
