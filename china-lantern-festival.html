Light & togetherness

元宵节

Lantern Festival

Yuánxiāo Jié

Lantern displays, riddles and rice-ball treats mark the closing chapter of the new-year celebrations.

February–March · typical season

The story

Lantern Festival brings the first lunar month’s celebrations to a bright finale. Looking at illuminated displays, trying lantern riddles and sharing glutinous rice balls are familiar customs. The festival date and a commercial lantern exhibition’s opening dates are different things.

What you may experience

  • Lantern displays with decorative scenes and figures.
  • Riddles attached to lanterns or offered through local activities.
  • In Shanghai, Yuyuan Garden Malls is a documented lantern-festival setting.

Food & shared tables

Tangyuan and yuanxiao are glutinous rice-ball treats associated with the festival. Fillings and preparation vary; ask about allergens.

Local starting points

Shanghai · Yuyuan Garden Malls

Visiting thoughtfully

Editorial planning suggestions

  • Plan your return journey before entering an evening display.
  • Follow crowd routes and avoid blocking narrow passages for photographs.
  • Ask before touching lanterns or entering workshops.

When it happens

Fifteenth day of the first month of the Chinese calendar. Lantern exhibitions may open for longer periods.
