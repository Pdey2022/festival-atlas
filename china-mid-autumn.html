Countries / China / Mid-Autumn Festival

Moonlight & reunion

中秋节

Mid-Autumn Festival

Zhōngqiū Jié

Moon-viewing and shared mooncakes celebrate reunion and time with family.

September–October · typical season

The story

Mid-Autumn Festival places the moon at the centre of a family occasion. Sharing mooncakes and looking at the night sky are familiar practices. Stories such as Chang’e’s journey to the moon are part of the festival’s cultural imagination.

What you may experience

  • Mooncake tasting and seasonal food displays.
  • Moon-viewing where access, weather and local conditions allow.
  • Public cultural activities advertised by city or venue organisers.

Food & shared tables

Mooncakes are shared and given as gifts. Fillings vary widely; check ingredients and portion sizes when tasting.
