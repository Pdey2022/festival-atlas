Countries / China / Qingming Festival

Remembrance & spring

清明节

Qingming Festival

Qīngmíng Jié

A time for remembering ancestors, caring for family graves and noticing the return of spring.

Early April · typical season

The story

Qingming is an occasion for remembrance. Families may visit and tend ancestral graves and pay their respects. Its spring setting also appears in seasonal foods. A respectful visitor’s experience should leave space for mourning and private family rituals.

What you may experience

  • Learning about remembrance customs through public cultural programmes.
  • Seasonal qingtuan in Shanghai and the surrounding lower-Yangtze food tradition.
  • A spring outing can complement the visit, without treating a burial ground as an attraction.

Food & shared tables

Qingtuan are green, chewy rice cakes eaten around Qingming. Shanghai shops offer sweet and savoury fillings; availability is seasonal.

Visiting thoughtfully

Editorial planning suggestions

  • Do not photograph mourners, offerings or graves without permission.
  • Choose public cultural activities rather than approaching private ceremonies.
  • Respect site rules for incense, offerings and access.
