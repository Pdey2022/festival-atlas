Countries / China / Spring Festival

Reunion & renewal

春节

Spring Festival

Chūnjié

Family reunions, new-year greetings and community celebrations welcome a fresh beginning.

January–February · typical season

The story

Spring Festival centres on renewing relationships and welcoming the new year. Families prepare their homes and food, gather on New Year’s Eve and exchange greetings. UNESCO describes traditions passed between generations through families, communities and craftspeople; practices differ across places and households.

What you may experience

  • Community performances and festive decorations, where publicly advertised.
  • In Beijing, temple fairs bring together food stalls, crafts and performing arts.
  • Family reunions form the heart of the celebration; they are usually private.

Food & shared tables

Food is prepared for shared family meals. Menus vary by region and household; ask local hosts or vendors about ingredients.

Local starting points

Beijing · temple fairs

Ditan and Longtan are examples documented by the city’s cultural portal. Check the year-specific programme, ticketing and opening arrangements.

Visiting thoughtfully

Editorial planning suggestions

  • Choose a public fair or performance for your visit; attend family gatherings only by invitation.
  • Check the organiser’s entry arrangements before setting out.
  • Leave room in your itinerary for queues and changes to local opening hours.

When it happens

First day of the first month of the Chinese calendar. Preparations begin earlier, and community celebrations run on their own schedules.
