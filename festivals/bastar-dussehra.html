Bastar Dussehra

बस्तर दशहरा

A long cycle of rituals honours Goddess Danteshwari, bringing together local communities, ceremonial chariots and regional deities.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Bastar Dussehra is a long cycle of observances centred on Danteshwari and the deity traditions of Bastar. Unlike many other Dussehra celebrations, it does not centre on burning an effigy of Ravana. Village deities and their representatives gather, while chariot-making connects skilled craft, faith and collective participation. The cycle extends far beyond the main procession days, so choosing a particular ritual is more useful than assuming there is one short festival weekend.

What you’ll experience

  • Pat Jatra associated with wood for the chariot
  • Crafting the ceremonial wooden chariot
  • Gatherings of deities and chariot processions

Food & festive dress

Explore regional food through public vendors or a local host. Ritual offerings and private community meals follow their own rules.

Dress respectfully and keep hands free in crowds. Community regalia and deity objects should never be handled without permission.

Where to experience it

Jagdalpur

Main town associated with Bastar Dussehra; procession routes are programme-dependent.

Visiting respectfully

  • Choose authorised viewing areas and keep clear of chariot ropes and procession routes.
  • Ask a local guide to explain the specific stage you are seeing rather than applying another region’s Dussehra story.
