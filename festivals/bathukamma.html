Bathukamma

బతుకమ్మ

Women arrange seasonal flowers in tiered stacks and gather around them to sing and celebrate.

View calendar

The story

Bathukamma celebrates seasonal flowers and the goddess through a distinctly Telangana tradition. Women arrange tiered floral stacks and gather around them to sing, before concluding celebrations at local water bodies.

What you’ll experience

  • Building layered flower arrangements
  • Singing in circles around the flowers
  • Local processions and immersion traditions

Food & festive dress

Shared offerings vary by day; maleeda is associated with celebrations.

Festive saris and regional textiles are common; observe local guidance when joining a gathering.

Where to experience it

Hyderabad

Public gatherings and neighbourhood celebrations; venues vary each year.

  • Use designated viewing areas and protect the flowers and waterside space.
