Bohag Bihu

বহাগ বিহু

Also called Rongali Bihu, this spring celebration welcomes the Assamese New Year with Bihu dance, music and festive meals.

View calendar

The story

Bohag, or Rongali, Bihu marks the Assamese New Year and the arrival of the agricultural spring. It is distinct from Magh Bihu after the harvest and Kati Bihu later in the farming cycle.

What you’ll experience

  • Bihu songs and dancing
  • Husori group performances
  • Family visits and exchanges of gamosa cloths

Food & festive dress

Pitha and laru are familiar festive foods; recipes differ between communities.

Mekhela chador and gamosa are associated with Assamese celebrations.
