Bonalu

బోనాలు

Devotees offer decorated pots of food to the goddess Mahakali, accompanied by local processions.

View calendar

The story

Bonalu is a Telangana festival of devotion and thanksgiving to Mahakali. In Hyderabad, Secunderabad and other communities, women carry decorated pots containing a prepared food offering to the goddess. The name is associated with the meal offered, and the procession combines devotion with strong neighbourhood participation. Celebrations take place through the month of Ashadam, with different temples and localities following their own schedules. A visitor should therefore choose a particular temple programme rather than assume there is one citywide festival day.

What you’ll experience

  • Preparing and carrying the bonam offering in decorated pots
  • Temple worship dedicated to Mahakali
  • Neighbourhood processions and accompanying music

Food & festive dress

The bonam is a sacred food offering; descriptions include cooked rice, jaggery and curd. Preparations vary, and offerings are not general visitor refreshments.

Women carrying offerings often wear festive traditional attire. Visitors should dress respectfully and leave room for worshippers.

Visiting respectfully

  • Check the schedule for the particular Hyderabad or Secunderabad temple you wish to visit.
  • Avoid standing in the procession’s path or interrupting someone carrying an offering for a photograph.
