01 · The story

The story

Goa’s Carnival, often written Carnaval, is a public celebration with processions, music, costumes and decorated floats. Its history is linked to the pre-Lenten carnival tradition, with a distinctive Goan expression today. Parades in different towns form separate programmes rather than one continuously moving event. The figure of King Momo is associated with the festivities. Carnival is distinct from Shigmo, another major Goan celebration with its own seasonal and cultural roots.

What you’ll experience

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Food vendors and nearby restaurants offer varied Goan dishes. Ask about seafood, meat and vegetarian ingredients rather than assuming a uniform carnival menu.

Costumes belong to participating groups; spectators can wear light, comfortable clothes and walking shoes.
