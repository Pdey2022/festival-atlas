Chhath Puja

छठ पूजा

Devotees offer prayers to the setting and rising sun at rivers and ponds, with fasting and carefully prepared offerings.

View calendar

The story

Chhath is an observance of gratitude to the sun and Chhathi Maiya. It is especially associated with Bihar and neighbouring regions, and is also observed by communities far from their home states.

What you’ll experience

  • Fasting and carefully prepared offerings
  • Prayers to the setting sun
  • Prayers at sunrise near water

Food & festive dress

Thekua and fruit are among the offerings; preparation follows household religious practices.

Dress respectfully and keep worship areas clear.

Where to experience it

Patna

Designated river ghats; access arrangements change by year.

Visiting respectfully

  • Do not interrupt fasting devotees or step across offerings.
  • Stay behind waterside barriers; ask before close-up photographs.
