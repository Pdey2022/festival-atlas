01 · The story

The story

Dev Deepawali transforms Varanasi’s riverfront with rows of lamps on Kartik Purnima. It is a separate observance from the main Diwali night, with worship, prayers and cultural activity focused on the Ganga and its ghats. The illuminated steps are visually striking, but they remain working public and sacred spaces. A riverside walk, a fixed viewing point and a boat trip offer very different experiences; choose one that suits your group’s mobility and comfort with crowds. The exact event programme and access arrangements change by edition.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Lighting lamps along the ghats
  • Prayers and riverfront religious observances
  • Cultural music and performances where scheduled

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Varanasi’s local snacks and sweets can be explored away from the busiest viewing areas. Festival offerings should be handled only as directed by organisers.

Wear comfortable shoes with grip for steps and respectful clothing around worship areas. Carry a light evening layer.
