Ganesh Chaturthi

गणेश चतुर्थी

Ganesha idols are welcomed into homes and public pandals, with prayers, modak sweets and immersion processions.

View calendar

The story

Ganesh Chaturthi welcomes Ganesha into homes and public pandals. Maharashtra's community celebrations combine worship, craftsmanship and collective organisation. Household observances vary in duration, so immersion days are not identical everywhere.

What you’ll experience

  • Installation of Ganesha idols
  • Aarti and offerings
  • Visarjan immersion processions

Food & festive dress

Modak sweets are strongly associated with Ganesha; recipes and offerings vary.

Respectful, comfortable clothing is suitable; follow temple or pandal instructions.
