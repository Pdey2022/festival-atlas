01 · The story

The story

Ganesh Chaturthi welcomes Ganesha into homes and public pandals. Maharashtra's community celebrations combine worship, craftsmanship and collective organisation. Household observances vary in duration, so immersion days are not identical everywhere.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Installation of Ganesha idols
  • Aarti and offerings
  • Visarjan immersion processions

How the celebration unfolds

A typical sequence, not confirmed annual dates. Local observances and public programmes vary.

  1. Installation

    Ganesha idols are installed in homes and community pandals.

  2. Days of worship

    Aarti, offerings and community activities continue. Households and mandals may keep different observance lengths.

  3. Immersion

    Processions accompany the farewell. Some take place earlier; Anant Chaturdashi marks the major closing observance. Confirm the chosen mandal’s schedule.

A suggested visit

Adapt these outings to the confirmed local programme and your preferred pace.

  1. First outing

    Visit one mandal, leaving time for the queue and a food stop.

  2. Optional second outing

    Choose another local celebration or a permitted viewing area after checking the current programme.

Getting around

Check rail or Metro options for the main journey, then confirm walking access to the mandal. On procession days, agree a pickup outside the affected area rather than expecting door-to-door vehicle access.

Visiting with children

Choose a short pandal visit with a clear exit plan. Ask about queue lengths before bringing children, and consider an earlier visit instead of an immersion procession.

Accessibility questions to ask

Ask about step-free entrances, seating, queue assistance and accessible toilets. Check whether a special entrance exists before arrival; do not assume priority access is available.

Facilities and routes have not been audited. Confirm your specific needs directly with the venue.

Small details that help

  • Ask before taking photographs during aarti.
  • Watch immersions from designated public areas and keep clear of working procession routes.
