Gangaur

गणगौर

Women honour Gauri with rituals, decorated images and colourful processions.

View calendar

The story

Gangaur honours Gauri and is especially associated with women's devotional traditions in Rajasthan. Udaipur's Mewar Festival overlaps with these celebrations, giving the occasion a distinctive lakeside expression.

What you’ll experience

  • Worship of decorated images
  • Songs and processions
  • Local cultural programmes

Food & festive dress

Seek regional sweets and seasonal dishes; customs vary between families and towns.

Colourful saris and regional dress are common in processions.

Where to experience it
