Gudi Padwa

गुढीपाडवा

Families raise decorated gudis outside their homes to welcome the new year.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Gudi Padwa welcomes the Marathi New Year at the beginning of Chaitra. A gudi, raised outside the home, gives the celebration its distinctive visual symbol and expresses auspiciousness and renewal. Household prayers and meals sit alongside public shobhayatra processions in places such as Pune and Mumbai. It shares a seasonal setting with other regional New Year celebrations, but its customs and local programmes deserve their own attention.

What you’ll experience

  • Raising and worshipping a decorated gudi
  • Making rangoli and decorating entrances
  • Community shobhayatra processions where announced

Food & festive dress

Puran poli and shrikhand are familiar festive foods. Neem and jaggery can express acceptance of life’s bitter and sweet experiences; customs vary.

Saris, kurtas and regional festive clothing are common. Comfortable footwear is useful for public procession viewing.

Where to experience it

Pune

Public processions and household traditions; check neighbourhood-specific schedules.

Visiting respectfully

  • View household gudis from public space unless invited inside.
  • Confirm the route and time of the particular shobhayatra, and leave clear space for performers and worshippers.
