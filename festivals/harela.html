Harela

हरेला

Seedlings grown in the days before the festival symbolise greenery, prosperity and the agricultural season.

View calendar

The story

Harela celebrates greenery and the close relationship between people and the agricultural landscape of Uttarakhand. The monsoon observance is especially associated with Kumaon, while contemporary celebrations across the state include tree planting and environmental activities. The festival offers a useful introduction to living local traditions beyond the better-known pilgrimage routes. A household ceremony, village gathering and organised plantation event may each emphasise different aspects. Visitors interested in joining should choose a publicly announced activity and learn from the organisers about its local meaning.

What you’ll experience

  • Seasonal celebrations connected with greenery and agriculture
  • Family or community observances according to local custom
  • Tree-planting programmes organised by community and public bodies

Food & festive dress

Meals depend on the household or event. Try regional Kumaoni food through a local host, without assuming that one dish defines every Harela celebration.

Comfortable clothing suitable for outdoor activity is useful. For planting programmes, use footwear that can cope with mud.
