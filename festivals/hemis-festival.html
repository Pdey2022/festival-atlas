Hemis Festival

Hemis Tsechu

Masked cham dances and monastery rituals commemorate Guru Padmasambhava.

View calendar

The story

Hemis Festival honours Guru Padmasambhava at Hemis Monastery in Ladakh. Its cham dances are religious performances, not simply entertainment. The annual dates follow the Tibetan lunar calendar.

What you’ll experience

  • Masked cham dances
  • Monastery prayers
  • Ceremonial music

Food & festive dress

Explore local Ladakhi food outside the ritual spaces; follow monastery rules on eating.

Modest layers suit monastery visits and changeable mountain conditions.
