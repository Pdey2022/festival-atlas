01 · The story

The story

Hemis Festival honours Guru Padmasambhava at Hemis Monastery in Ladakh. Its cham dances are religious performances, not simply entertainment. The annual dates follow the Tibetan lunar calendar.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Masked cham dances
  • Monastery prayers
  • Ceremonial music
