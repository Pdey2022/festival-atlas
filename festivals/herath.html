Devotion · Community

Herath

The Kashmiri observance of Shivratri centres on family worship, ritual offerings and festive food.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Herath is the Kashmiri Pandit observance of Shivaratri, with a distinctive sequence of household preparations, worship and social visits. The Indira Gandhi National Centre for the Arts documents the use of ritual vessels and walnuts in the ceremonies, followed by the sharing of soaked walnuts with relatives, friends and neighbours. It is primarily a living family and community tradition, not a single public spectacle. Kashmiri Pandit communities also maintain the celebration beyond the Kashmir Valley, so its cultural geography extends beyond any one destination.

What you’ll experience

  • Household worship involving ritual vessels
  • Preparation and later sharing of soaked walnuts
  • Visits and exchanges that renew family and neighbourhood ties

Food & festive dress

Walnuts have a specific ceremonial role. Other dishes vary by household, and a visitor should ask their host about the meal rather than assume one Shivaratri menu applies everywhere.

Respectful everyday or festive clothing is suitable when invited. Warm layers are useful for a winter visit to Srinagar.
