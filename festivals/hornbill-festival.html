Hornbill Festival

Hornbill Festival

Naga communities share performances, crafts, food and traditions at a major annual cultural gathering.

View calendar

The story

Organised as a cultural showcase since 2000, Hornbill brings together different Naga communities. It offers performances, crafts and food alongside contemporary events. The shared programme does not replace each community's own festivals or distinct traditions.

What you’ll experience

  • Cultural presentations at Kisama
  • Craft and textile demonstrations
  • Music and community food stalls

Food & festive dress

Stalls offer different Naga cuisines. Ask vendors about ingredients, spice levels and vegetarian options.

Community textiles and ornaments have specific meanings; ask before borrowing, handling or photographing them closely.

Where to experience it

Kisama Heritage Village

Main cultural showcase near Kohima; programme and access are organiser-dependent.
