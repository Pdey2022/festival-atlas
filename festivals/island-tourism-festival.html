Island Tourism Festival

A public cultural programme showcases island communities through performances, exhibitions and local food; confirm the organiser’s current schedule.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

The Island Tourism Festival is an organised cultural event in the Andaman and Nicobar Islands. It brings together performances, exhibitions and public entertainment, with visiting mainland troupes joining island-based cultural groups. This makes it different from a single community’s religious festival: its programme is curated by the organisers and can change from one edition to another. The principal urban venue has historically been the ITF Ground in the capital, now named Sri Vijaya Puram. Use the current official announcement to establish dates and venues rather than relying on the festival’s historic January pattern.

What you’ll experience

  • Stage programmes featuring music and dance
  • Exhibitions and stalls where included in the edition
  • Participation by island and visiting cultural groups

Food & festive dress

Food stalls depend on the published programme. Ask vendors about seafood, allergens and vegetarian choices; the event does not have one prescribed festival meal.

Light everyday clothing and comfortable footwear suit outdoor stalls; performers wear the clothing appropriate to their own traditions.
