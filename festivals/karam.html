Karam

करम

Community celebrations centre on the Karam tree, with songs and dances expressing hopes for wellbeing and prosperity.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Karam centres on reverence for Karam devta and is observed by several communities in Jharkhand and neighbouring regions. Young people, communal singing and dance are prominent in accounts of the celebration. Its relationship with nature, wellbeing and collective life makes the ritual setting important, not merely the performance. Practices vary, so a host’s explanation of their own observance is more reliable than assuming one statewide sequence.

What you’ll experience

  • Worship associated with Karam devta
  • Community songs and dance
  • Gatherings in which young people play an important role

Food & festive dress

Offerings and shared meals follow local custom. Ask hosts about preparation and participation rather than assuming every gathering has a public feast.

Regional festive textiles and everyday dress vary by community. Choose respectful clothing and avoid handling ceremonial objects.

Visiting respectfully

  • Ask which activities are public and where visitors may stand before a ceremony starts.
  • Seek permission for portraits and recordings, particularly when songs or rituals are not intended as public performances.
