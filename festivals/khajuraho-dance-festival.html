Arts · Music & dance

Khajuraho Dance Festival

Indian classical dance traditions are showcased against the backdrop of Khajuraho’s temple heritage.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Khajuraho Dance Festival presents Indian classical dance in the setting of Khajuraho’s monumental heritage. It is a curated arts programme, with named performers and scheduled presentations, rather than a single religious rite shared by all visitors. Different dance traditions bring distinctive movement, music and storytelling. Reading the programme before attending makes it easier to understand what you are watching and to choose performances that interest you.

What you’ll experience

  • Scheduled classical dance recitals
  • Musical accompaniment and expressive storytelling
  • Associated cultural activities when included by organisers

Food & festive dress

Food is part of the wider visitor experience rather than a defining ritual. Plan a meal around performance times and use the designated eating areas.

Comfortable, respectful evening clothing is suitable for audiences. Performers’ costumes belong to particular dance traditions.

Visiting respectfully

  • Check the current performance roster, venue and entry arrangements; an earlier edition’s lineup is not a promise.
  • Keep phones silent and follow rules on flash, filming and movement during a recital; visit the monuments separately if required.
