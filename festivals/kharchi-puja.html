Devotion · Community

Kharchi Puja

The Fourteen Gods are honoured through rituals and a public fair at the Chaturdasha Devata temple.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Kharchi Puja is one of Tripura’s major religious festivals, centred on the Fourteen Gods temple at Old Agartala. Its traditions connect the state’s historic royal worship with a much wider gathering of devotees and visitors. Ceremonies and a surrounding fair bring different communities together over several days. For a visitor, the temple observance and the public fair are related but distinct spaces: worshippers take priority around sacred objects and ritual routes. The lunar calendar and local organisers determine the programme each year.

What you’ll experience

  • Worship associated with the fourteen deities
  • Ceremonial activities led by the temple’s religious authorities
  • A public fair and community gathering around the festival

Food & festive dress

Sweets and other offerings have devotional roles. Food sold at the fair is a separate visitor choice; ask about ingredients and serving arrangements.

Respectful clothing suitable for temple surroundings is appropriate. Follow instructions about footwear and restricted areas.
