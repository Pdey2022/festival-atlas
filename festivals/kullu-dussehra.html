01 · The story

The story

Kullu Dussehra brings local deities and their representatives together around Lord Raghunath. A ceremonial chariot journey into Dhalpur forms a central public moment, followed by a wider fair and cultural programme. The gathering expresses relationships between valley communities as well as devotion. Its sequence differs from familiar one-day Dussehra spectacles elsewhere, so the arrival of deities, processions and performances should be planned as distinct experiences.

06 · Visiting respectfully

Visiting respectfully

  • Keep clear of chariot ropes, bearers and deity palanquins even when taking photographs.
  • Check the current Dhalpur programme and allow extra travel time around procession closures.
