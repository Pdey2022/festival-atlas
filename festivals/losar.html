Community · Devotion

Losar

Monpa communities welcome the new year with monastery prayers, family visits and traditional food.

View calendar

The story

Losar is a New Year celebration within Tibetan Buddhist cultural traditions. In Tawang it is especially important to Monpa communities; it is also observed in Sikkim and other Himalayan regions. Religious prayers, family reunions and meals accompany hopes for a fortunate year. Local calendars and customs differ, so this guide does not assign one universal date or programme. Tawang’s Torgya monastery festival is a separate occasion and should not be confused with Losar.

What you’ll experience

  • New Year prayers at home and religious venues
  • Visits and reunions with relatives
  • Sharing festive meals and greetings

Food & festive dress

Traditional feasts form part of the celebration. Regional foods and household recipes differ; ask hosts what their dishes signify.

Wear modest layers for monastery visits. Community ceremonial clothing is meaningful rather than a required visitor costume.

Where to experience it

Visiting respectfully

  • Confirm the community’s own calendar before arranging travel, especially when comparing Himalayan destinations.
  • Ask separately about monastery access and household invitations; a family celebration may not offer a public performance.
Accessibility & practical planning

Step-free routes, toilets, seating, quiet spaces and crowd controls have not been audited. Ask the venue about your specific needs and check transport arrangements before visiting.
