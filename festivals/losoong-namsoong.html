Harvest · Community · Devotion

Losoong / Namsoong

Bhutia and Lepcha communities mark seasonal renewal through their distinct traditions and celebrations.

View calendar

The story

Losoong and Namsoong are closely associated seasonal celebrations in Sikkim, observed among Bhutia and Lepcha communities respectively. The period celebrates the harvest and includes family gatherings, while associated monastery programmes may feature ceremonial cham dances. The Government of Sikkim also notes traditional skills such as archery. These related observances should not erase differences between communities or be confused with Losar. A monastery’s dance programme can take place before the main family festival, making local confirmation especially important for visitors.

What you’ll experience

  • Family and community harvest celebrations
  • Cham dances at participating monasteries on their own schedules
  • Traditional games or archery where organised

Food & festive dress

Family meals reflect local traditions and preferences. Ask hosts about the menu; a regional dish available in Gangtok is not automatically a ceremonial food.

Traditional Bhutia and Lepcha clothing may be seen at celebrations. Bring warm layers for winter outdoor viewing.

Visiting respectfully

  • Confirm the specific monastery’s programme rather than assuming all dances happen on the same day.
  • Remain outside the ritual performance area and follow monastery photography rules.
Accessibility & practical planning

Step-free routes, toilets, seating, quiet spaces and crowd controls have not been audited. Ask the venue about your specific needs and check transport arrangements before visiting.
