Devotion · Community · Arts

Mandi Shivratri

मंडी शिवरात्रि

Local deities, devotees and performers gather for a celebration associated with Maha Shivratri.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Mandi Shivratri combines religious observances with a major gathering of local deities, processions and cultural events. Bhootnath Temple and the presence of Madho Rai are important within its ceremonial setting. The fair gives visitors an opportunity to understand how village deity traditions relate to the town. Evening performances, exhibitions and the religious sequence are different parts of the occasion; they may operate on separate schedules.

What you’ll experience

  • Gathering of local deities and their attendants
  • Religious visits and ceremonial processions
  • Cultural evenings, exhibitions and public fair activities

Food & festive dress

Himachali dham is associated with regional celebrations and is mentioned in tourism accounts of the fair. Confirm where meals are offered and how to join.

Respectful layers suit temple visits and outdoor evenings. Colourful local turbans are part of procession dress.
