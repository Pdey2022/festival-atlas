Harvest · Community · Arts

Moatsu

After sowing, Ao communities celebrate with songs, dances and communal gatherings.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Moatsu is an Ao Naga festival associated with community bonding after agricultural work. The Ministry of Tourism describes a three-day celebration in May, bringing people together through shared festivities. Its importance lies in the relationships renewed among neighbours and families, alongside cultural expression. Mokokchung and surrounding Ao villages provide the relevant geographical context, but an announced public event and a village’s own observance may have different arrangements. Treat the general season as an introduction and seek the host community’s programme before making a special journey.

What you’ll experience

  • Community gatherings and shared celebrations
  • Traditional music and dance in organised programmes
  • Hospitality that renews relationships among participants

Food & festive dress

Food is arranged by the host community; ask what is included before accepting a meal, particularly if you avoid meat or particular ingredients.

Ao shawls and traditional clothing may be worn by participants. Ceremonial garments have meanings that a local guide can explain.

Where to experience it
