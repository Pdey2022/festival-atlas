Mysuru Dasara

ಮೈಸೂರು ದಸರಾ

An illuminated palace, performances and the celebrated procession make Dasara a signature Mysuru experience.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Mysuru Dasara combines worship of Chamundeshwari with the city's royal and public cultural heritage. Palace illumination and performances build towards the festival's celebrated procession. Specific programmes can have separate tickets.

What you’ll experience

  • Palace illumination
  • Classical and folk performances
  • The ceremonial Dasara procession

Food & festive dress

Mysore pak is a local sweet to try; it is not exclusive to Dasara.

Comfortable clothing works for public viewing; follow dress requirements in religious venues.
