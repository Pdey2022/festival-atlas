Navratri

નવરાત્રી

Nine nights of devotion to the goddess are celebrated with circular garba dancing and dandiya raas.

View calendar

The story

Navratri honours the goddess over nine nights. Gujarat's garba circles and dandiya gatherings are especially well known, while other regions have distinct devotional and artistic traditions. This guide focuses on the autumn celebration.

What you’ll experience

  • Garba and dandiya raas
  • Prayers and household observances
  • Community gatherings over successive evenings

Food & festive dress

Fasting and festive menus differ. Ask hosts about the customs of the event you attend.

Chaniya cholis and kediyu outfits are associated with Gujarati celebrations; comfortable footwear and freedom of movement matter.
