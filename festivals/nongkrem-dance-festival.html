01 · The story

The story

Nongkrem is a Khasi thanksgiving festival held at Smit, the traditional seat of Hima Khyrim near Shillong. Its ceremonies connect prayers for prosperity and a good harvest with the responsibilities of the Syiem and religious authorities. The public dance is one part of a larger sacred observance, rather than a performance arranged simply for visitors. Women and men have distinct roles, and the ceremonial dress, music and movement carry community meanings. Autumn is a useful planning window, but the organising authorities announce the actual programme.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Thanksgiving ceremonies associated with the Pomblang observance
  • Women and men performing ceremonial dances in the courtyard
  • Traditional instrumental music accompanying the dancers

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Look for locally run Khasi food stalls nearby; refreshments for visitors are separate from ceremonial offerings. Ask about ingredients and vegetarian options.

Dancers wear elaborate Khasi ceremonial textiles and ornaments. Visitors can wear comfortable, respectful everyday clothing.

06 · Visiting respectfully

Visiting respectfully

  • Stay outside the designated dancing space and ask before photographing ceremonial participants.
  • Confirm the principal dance day before arranging transport from Shillong; allow time for traffic near Smit.
