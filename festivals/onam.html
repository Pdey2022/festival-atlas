01 · The story

The story

Onam celebrates seasonal abundance and the legendary return of King Mahabali. It brings Malayali families and communities together through floral designs, hospitality and performances. Public programmes and household traditions have different schedules.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Pookalam floral designs
  • An Onasadya meal served on a banana leaf
  • Cultural performances and community gatherings

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Sadya is a vegetarian feast with rice, vegetable dishes and payasam; menus vary by household and venue.

Kerala kasavu textiles are often worn for celebrations. Comfortable, respectful clothing is suitable for visitors.

Before you book

Duration, costs & booking

Festival duration
Traditionally a ten-day celebration beginning with Atham; individual performances and public programmes have their own dates.
Suggested visit · editorial estimate
2–3 days around a chosen public celebration and an Onasadya meal.
Costs to plan for
Restaurant sadya meals, boat-race seating and cultural shows may have separate charges. Current menus and ticket prices are not verified here.
Booking checklist · planning advice
Reserve a sadya sitting with the restaurant and check dietary needs directly. Match any boat race or performance to its own published programme, rather than assuming it coincides with Thiruvonam.
