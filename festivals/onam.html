The story

Onam celebrates seasonal abundance and the legendary return of King Mahabali. It brings Malayali families and communities together through floral designs, hospitality and performances. Public programmes and household traditions have different schedules.

What you’ll experience

  • Pookalam floral designs
  • An Onasadya meal served on a banana leaf
  • Cultural performances and community gatherings

Food & festive dress

Sadya is a vegetarian feast with rice, vegetable dishes and payasam; menus vary by household and venue.

Kerala kasavu textiles are often worn for celebrations. Comfortable, respectful clothing is suitable for visitors.

  • Boat races are separate events: verify their dates independently.
