Rath Yatra

ରଥଯାତ୍ରା

The deities Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra travel in monumental wooden chariots through Puri.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Puri's Rath Yatra brings Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra into a public chariot procession. The journey forms part of a larger cycle of observances, including the return journey, which must be checked separately.

What you’ll experience

  • Wooden chariot construction
  • The outward chariot procession
  • Later return observances

Food & festive dress

Mahaprasad is associated with Jagannath traditions; seek guidance on authorised distribution.

Wear respectful clothing and practical footwear for long periods outdoors.

Where to experience it

Puri

Grand Road procession corridor; access follows local arrangements.
