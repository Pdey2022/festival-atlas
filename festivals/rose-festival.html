Rose Festival

Flower displays and cultural activities celebrate the city’s garden heritage.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Chandigarh’s Rose Festival is an organised civic celebration built around the city’s rose gardens and seasonal blooms. It combines horticultural interest with a public festival atmosphere, and the tourism administration usually places it in the late-February period. Visitors can approach it as a garden outing, while any entertainment, competitions or additional attractions depend on that edition’s programme. The Rose Garden is the main geographical reference, but organisers may use surrounding public spaces as well. Confirm the official announcement before assuming a particular activity or opening time.

What you’ll experience

  • Viewing rose displays and garden plantings
  • Public cultural activities where announced
  • Horticultural interest and community participation

Food & festive dress

Refreshments depend on approved stalls and nearby businesses. There is no ritual menu; bring drinking water and ask about ingredients at food counters.

Comfortable daytime clothing and walking shoes work well. A light layer may be useful for an evening programme.
