Sangai Festival

A tourism festival presents the state’s dance, crafts, food and cultural traditions.

View calendar

The story

Sangai Festival is an organised showcase of Manipur’s arts, crafts, cuisine and sporting traditions. It takes its name from the state’s distinctive brow-antlered deer. Past editions have brought performances, handlooms and demonstrations together across several venues, helping visitors encounter the diversity of the state. This is an annual event whose delivery depends on organiser announcements: a historical programme should not be presented as confirmation of a forthcoming edition.

What you’ll experience

  • Announced dance and music performances
  • Handloom and craft exhibitions
  • Demonstrations of indigenous games when programmed

Food & festive dress

Past food programmes have included dishes such as eromba, singju and fish curry. Ingredients vary; ask specifically about fermented fish and vegetarian preparation.

Comfortable clothing suits exhibition grounds. Performance costumes represent distinct communities rather than one universal Manipuri outfit.

Where to experience it

Imphal

Planning base; festival venues may also be outside the city.

Visiting respectfully

  • Confirm that the edition is going ahead, then check venues, tickets and local travel arrangements.
  • Plan transport between venues instead of assuming all activities occur in one fairground; ask makers before photographing their work.
Accessibility & practical planning

Step-free routes, toilets, seating, quiet spaces and crowd controls have not been audited. Ask the venue about your specific needs and check transport arrangements before visiting.
