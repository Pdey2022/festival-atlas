01 · The story

The story

Sarhul welcomes spring among several Adivasi communities in Jharkhand. Sal blossoms are closely associated with the occasion, linking worship and community life to the changing season. Songs, dancing and gatherings express renewal, but names, narratives and ritual details differ between communities. A public city procession offers one way to encounter Sarhul; it should not be treated as a complete representation of every village’s sacred practices.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Families prepare celebratory food according to their own traditions. Ask before accepting or photographing offerings, and check ingredients with your host.

White saris with red borders appear in public celebrations, among other regional clothing. Visitors do not need to adopt community dress.

06 · Visiting respectfully

Visiting respectfully

  • Ask permission before entering a sacred grove or approaching a ritual area.
  • Use a local guide or host to identify the community and meaning of the celebration rather than describing all practices as the same.
