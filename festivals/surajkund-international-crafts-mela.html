Surajkund International Crafts Mela

Artisans and performers gather for a major cultural fair showcasing handicrafts, folk traditions and regional food.

View calendar

The story

Surajkund International Crafts Mela is an organised fair in Faridabad that brings craftspeople, weavers and performing groups together. Its central purpose is to give artisans a place to demonstrate and sell their work directly. Textiles, pottery and many other craft traditions appear alongside cultural performances. Themes and participating regions change between editions, so an old brochure is useful background but does not guarantee a particular stall or show this year.

What you’ll experience

  • Browsing artisan and handloom stalls
  • Watching craft demonstrations where offered
  • Folk performances at the mela’s open-air stages

Food & festive dress

The fair offers a variety of regional food choices. Check individual vendors for ingredients and dietary needs; no single cuisine represents the event.

Comfortable walking shoes and weather-appropriate clothing are more useful than formal dress for exploring the large grounds.

Where to experience it

Faridabad

City-centre reference; the mela itself is at Surajkund, with designated gates.

Visiting respectfully

  • Ask makers about materials, technique and care instructions before buying.
  • Check official ticketing, gate access and the performance schedule; allow time to return to the correct exit.
Accessibility & practical planning

Step-free routes, toilets, seating, quiet spaces and crowd controls have not been audited. Ask the venue about your specific needs and check transport arrangements before visiting.
