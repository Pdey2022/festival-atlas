Community · Devotion

Tamil Puthandu

புத்தாண்டு

Temple visits, festive meals and decorated entrances welcome the Tamil New Year.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Puthandu marks the opening of the Tamil month of Chithirai and the beginning of the Tamil New Year. It is a time for family visits, greetings and renewal, with household decorations and worship forming part of many celebrations. Kolam designs at entrances welcome the occasion, while special meals bring generations together. Tamil communities around the world observe the festival, and households vary in how they combine religious and cultural customs. In Tamil Nadu, a temple visit can form part of the day, but each temple sets its own arrangements.

What you’ll experience

  • Kolam designs and decorated household entrances
  • New Year greetings and seeking elders’ blessings
  • Special worship and family meals

Food & festive dress

Mango pachadi is a well-known seasonal preparation; festive meals can also include rice, vegetable dishes, vadai and payasam. Household recipes differ.

New or festive clothing is common. Follow the individual temple’s dress requirements if you plan to enter.
