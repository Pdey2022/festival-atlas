01 · The story

The story

This guide focuses on Haryana’s monsoon Teej traditions. The festival connects devotion to Parvati and Shiva with family relationships and the relief brought by seasonal rain. Women’s gatherings, songs, dance, mehendi and swings are familiar expressions of the occasion. Teej is observed in several regions and includes differently named observances; a public celebration in Haryana need not share the ritual calendar or procession format of Jaipur.

02 · Rituals & celebration

What you’ll experience

  • Women’s songs and dance gatherings
  • Mehendi and decorated swings
  • Prayers and family observances, including fasting for some participants

03 · Food & festive dress

Food & festive dress

Festive foods and sweets accompany social gatherings, while fasting practices vary. Ask hosts before offering food to someone observing a fast.

Colourful festive clothing and jewellery are common. Visitors can dress respectfully without copying personal or ceremonial adornment.
