Uttarayan

ઉત્તરાયણ

Rooftops fill with kite flyers during Makar Sankranti, with festive food and colourful skies.

View calendar

The story

Uttarayan is closely associated with kite flying across Gujarat around Makar Sankranti. Rooftops and open grounds become social spaces where families and friends watch the sky and share food. Ahmedabad’s organised International Kite Festival adds a public showcase of imaginative kites and visiting flyers. That scheduled event is related to, but not identical with, everyday neighbourhood celebrations; its venue and dates need separate confirmation.

What you’ll experience

  • Kite flying and watching from open spaces
  • Neighbourhood and family gatherings
  • Demonstrations at the organised International Kite Festival

Food & festive dress

Seasonal food is part of gatherings, but menus vary by household. Ask a local host about their favourite winter dishes and dietary ingredients.

Wear comfortable clothes, sun protection and secure footwear. Rooftop access is not a reason to approach unprotected edges.

Where to experience it

Ahmedabad

Public kite-festival programmes and neighbourhood celebrations; verify the current venue.

Visiting respectfully

  • Choose a public viewing ground or an explicitly hosted rooftop visit.
  • Avoid hazardous kite string and never retrieve kites near traffic or power lines; follow local organiser guidance.
Accessibility & practical planning

Step-free routes, toilets, seating, quiet spaces and crowd controls have not been audited. Ask the venue about your specific needs and check transport arrangements before visiting.
