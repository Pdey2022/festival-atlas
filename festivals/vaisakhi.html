Vaisakhi

ਵਿਸਾਖੀ

Harvest celebrations coincide with the commemoration of the formation of the Khalsa, with gurdwara visits and community meals.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Vaisakhi brings together Punjab's harvest season and the Sikh commemoration of the formation of the Khalsa. Gurdwara visits, service and community meals are important, alongside local fairs and cultural programmes.

What you’ll experience

  • Gurdwara worship and kirtan
  • Community langar
  • Local fairs and processions

Food & festive dress

Langar is a communal meal grounded in service and equality; follow the volunteers' guidance.

Cover your head and remove shoes where required in a gurdwara.
