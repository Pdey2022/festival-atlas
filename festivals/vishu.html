Vishu

വിഷു

The day begins with viewing the auspicious Vishukkani arrangement, followed by gifts and family meals.

View calendar

The story

Vishu marks an auspicious solar-calendar occasion in Kerala, usually during April. The first sight of the day, Vishukkani, is central: an arrangement of produce, flowers, a mirror and other meaningful objects is prepared with care. Gifts from elders and a shared feast connect the ritual to family life. Household practices and temple observances differ, and Vishu should not be treated as interchangeable with Onam or every other regional New Year.

What you’ll experience

  • Viewing the Vishukkani arrangement on waking
  • Vishukkaineetam gifts from elders
  • Family meals and temple visits where observed

Food & festive dress

Vishu sadya offers a range of Kerala dishes; menus vary. Vishu katta is another regional preparation associated with the occasion.

Kerala saris and mundu are familiar festive choices. Temple dress requirements may be more specific, so check before visiting.

Visiting respectfully

  • A household Vishukkani is an intimate family moment: arrange a host’s invitation rather than arriving unannounced.
  • If booking a festive meal, confirm its menu and service time independently of temple schedules.
