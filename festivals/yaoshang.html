Community · Music & dance

Yaoshang

A multiday spring celebration combines devotional traditions, local sports and thabal chongba dancing.

Plan your stayView calendar

The story

Yaoshang is an important spring celebration in Manipur, traditionally extending over several days around the full moon of Phalguna. It is especially associated with Meitei cultural life. Thabal Chongba, in which participants form a circle and dance together, is a recognisable part of the festive season. Community programmes vary, so the festival is best understood through a particular neighbourhood’s arrangements rather than a single assumed statewide timetable.

What you’ll experience

  • Community gatherings over the spring festive period
  • Thabal Chongba circle dancing
  • Local cultural activities according to the neighbourhood programme

Food & festive dress

Explore local food through public vendors or a host. Festival hospitality does not imply that every household meal is open to visitors.

Participants may wear Manipuri textiles and festive clothes. Ask hosts about appropriate clothing if invited to join an activity.
