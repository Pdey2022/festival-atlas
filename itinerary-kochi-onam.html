Four days of Onam around Kochi

Use one Kochi base for heritage walks, a booked sadya meal and time for a locally confirmed Onam celebration.

Your days, at a gentler pace

  1. Day 1 · Kochi

    Arrive and settle into Kochi

    Check in, confirm your sadya booking and choose a short walk near your accommodation. Ask your host which public Onam activities are actually announced for your dates.

    A more flexible option

    Keep this as a rest day if the journey is long or weather makes walking uncomfortable.

  2. Day 2 · Fort Kochi · Mattancherry

    Fort Kochi and Mattancherry

    Explore a manageable stretch of the heritage neighbourhoods, with a long lunch break. Choose one indoor attraction if it is open; check holiday hours and access before making the journey.

    A more flexible option

    Shorten the route to one neighbourhood and use the rest of the day for an indoor meal or gallery.

  3. Day 3 · Kochi or Thrikkakara

    Make time for Onam

    Enjoy a sadya with a confirmed provider. Consider Thrikkakara only after checking visitor access and the current programme; alternatively choose an announced public cultural event near your base.

    A more flexible option

    If public events are unavailable, keep the booked meal and a relaxed local day. Do not assume access to a private celebration.

  4. Day 4 · Kochi

    An unhurried departure

    Take a short local walk or browse nearby shops if time allows. Keep the last part of the day free for your station or airport transfer, checking the current service before leaving.

    A more flexible option

    Skip sightseeing when your departure is early or travel conditions are uncertain.

Getting around

Choose a Kochi base that suits your arrival and planned visits. Group Fort Kochi and Mattancherry together, and plan Thrikkakara separately. Check current road and ferry services; leave time for rain and holiday traffic.

Comfort and access

Build in indoor breaks and ask about steps, walking surfaces and seating at each venue. Confirm dietary needs directly with the sadya provider, including allergens and cross-contact.
