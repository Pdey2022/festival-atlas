Three days of Durga Puja in Kolkata

Take Kolkata one neighbourhood at a time: pandal art, a slower heritage morning and time to enjoy the city between celebrations.

Choose your timing

Choose three days within the locally announced Puja programme. The 2027 holiday reference covers Saptami–Navami only; it does not confirm individual pandal opening times.

Reorder these days to fit confirmed local events and your arrival. No performance, meal or attraction is booked by using this plan.

Your days, at a gentler pace

  1. Day 1 · Kumartuli and nearby neighbourhoods

    Arrive and explore north Kolkata

    Settle in, then choose a small cluster of north Kolkata pandals. If an artisan workshop welcomes visitors, ask permission before entering or taking photos. Leave space for queues and a meal.

    A more flexible option

    Arriving late? Keep to a single nearby pandal and move the longer walk to another day.

  2. Day 2 · South Kolkata

    A slower morning, then south Kolkata

    Rest in the morning. Choose a compact south Kolkata pandal circuit from the current local programme, with a meal and seated break between visits. Arrange your return before joining evening crowds.

    A more flexible option

    For a gentler day, ask organisers about daytime access and reduce the number of stops.

  3. Day 3 · Near your accommodation

    One last neighbourhood

    Return to a favourite local celebration or take a short neighbourhood walk, then leave a generous transfer buffer. Do not assume an immersion procession or river viewing point is open on your departure day.

    A more flexible option

    If streets are restricted, enjoy a nearby meal and prioritise the journey to your station or airport.

Getting around

Stay in one Kolkata base and group visits by neighbourhood. Check current Metro services, road closures and the final walk to each pandal before setting out. Keep your departure day flexible.

Comfort and access

Expect queues and walking. Ask individual organisers about step-free entry, toilets and quieter visiting periods; these routes have not been accessibility-audited.
