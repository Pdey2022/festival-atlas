Your days, at a gentler pace

  1. Day 1 · Your booked Kohima-area base

    Arrive and meet your host

    Transfer to your accommodation, rest and confirm the next day’s pickup. Review the actual festival programme with your host and choose only a few priorities.

    A more flexible option

    If the transfer is delayed, keep the evening free rather than trying to reach a scheduled performance.

  2. Day 2 · Kisama Heritage Village

    A first day at Kisama

    Choose cultural presentations from the current programme, with breaks for food and rest. Ask before photographing people or entering community spaces. Confirm your return pickup before leaving the vehicle.

    A more flexible option

    Shorten the visit to one session if crowds, weather or walking conditions are tiring.

  3. Day 3 · Kisama Heritage Village

    Return for something different

    Return for activities you missed, allowing time to explore craft and food areas. Check whether any evening music event uses another venue or requires a separate ticket.

    A more flexible option

    Take a quieter day at your base if the programme repeats or you need a break.

  4. Day 4 · Khonoma or Kohima

    A local excursion

    Consider a host-arranged Khonoma visit with a local guide, subject to access, road conditions and availability. Agree the route and return time first; otherwise keep a shorter Kohima outing.

    A more flexible option

    Stay near your accommodation when the excursion is impractical. Treat village visits as hosted encounters, not unrestricted access.

  5. Day 5 · Your onward route

    Leave a generous travel buffer

    Reserve the day for checkout and your onward transfer. Confirm the road situation and departure time with your driver; avoid adding a festival visit before a fixed flight or train.

    A more flexible option

    If the journey needs an extra overnight stop, extend your stay and update the sample budget.

Getting around

Agree arrival transport to your booked base and each Kisama return pickup in advance. Kohima can be a base, but confirm the exact accommodation address and current road conditions with your host. Keep arrival and departure days free of fixed festival commitments.

Comfort and access

Discuss gradients, steps, seating, toilets and drop-off distance with your host and venue. Ask about official entry requirements for your own circumstances before booking; this itinerary does not determine permit eligibility.
