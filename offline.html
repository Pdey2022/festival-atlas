Checking downloads…

    Open my trip →Open saved festivals →

    Choose your next download

    Updating replaces your existing offline selection. Trip notes stay in browser storage and are not uploaded. Adding a festival to your trip later does not automatically download its guide.

    Choose individual guides

    Only selected guides’ photos are downloaded. Without photos, guide text, the India map, dates and your trip remain available. External maps, source websites and photo licence pages need a connection.

    Before you set off

    1. Download while connected, then open one of the downloaded guide links above.
    2. Switch off your connection and check that the guide and My trip open.
    3. Keep a trip JSON backup or a printed copy. Browsers can remove downloaded data when storage is low or site data is cleared.

    The download time tells you when you saved a copy, not when a festival date was verified. Each guide and annual listing keeps its own review or check date. Reconnect and update your pack before travel.

    Downloads belong to this browser and device. They do not sync to other devices.
